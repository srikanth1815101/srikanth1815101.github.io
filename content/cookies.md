This Cookie Policy explains how CSRGO ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar client-side technologies when you visit our website at [https://csrgo.com](https://csrgo.com) and its associated subdomains across the CSRGO digital ecosystem (including tools.csrgo.com, dsa.csrgo.com, and blog.csrgo.com) (collectively, the "Site").

It describes what these technologies are, why we use them, and the choices you have to manage or disable them.

## 1. What Are Cookies and Web Storage?

*   **Cookies:** Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit a website. They are widely used by website operators to make websites function efficiently, retain session states, and provide analytical reporting.
*   **Local Storage:** HTML5 Local Storage (`localStorage`) allows web applications to store data locally within the user's browser without transferring that data across network requests. Data stored in `localStorage` persists until cleared by the user or web application.

## 2. Our Approach: No First-Party Tracking Cookies

Because CSRGO is built around a philosophy of **zero friction and client-side privacy**, we do not operate login accounts, member profiles, or user dashboards:

*   **We do not use first-party cookies to track your personal identity.**
*   **We do not track you across the web** using proprietary tracking scripts.
*   You can freely explore all developer tools, practice algorithmic problems, and read technical publications without ever needing a cookie-based login session.

## 3. How We Use Browser Local Storage

We utilize client-side HTML5 `localStorage` strictly for functional, non-identifiable interface preferences:

*   **Theme Preference:** We store your preferred display mode (`light` or `dark`) so the site renders your chosen color palette consistently across page navigations.
*   **Transient Tool Configurations:** Certain browser-based utilities on [tools.csrgo.com](https://tools.csrgo.com) (such as JSON indentation spaces or regex flag toggles) may persist your chosen options in local storage for convenience.
*   **Zero Server Sync:** This data remains strictly on your local device. It is never uploaded to, monitored by, or synchronized with any remote server.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies (Analytics & Advertising)

To keep CSRGO 100% free for developers and learners worldwide, we rely on trusted third-party services that may place cookies on your device:

### A. Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymized statistical data about how visitors interact with our Site (such as the number of visitors, most visited pages, average session duration, and device types). These cookies help us identify high-demand tools and improve platform performance. They do not identify you personally.

### B. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising
We display advertisements served by Google AdSense to fund server infrastructure, bandwidth, and ongoing software development:
*   Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
*   Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our sites and/or other sites across the internet.
*   These cookies allow advertisers to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.

### C. Hosting Infrastructure (GitHub Pages)
Our platform is statically hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may utilize essential technical cookies to ensure platform reliability, load balancing, and network security against automated cyber threats.

## 5. How You Can Control and Manage Cookies

You have the complete right to accept, reject, or delete cookies at any time:

*   **Browser Controls:** Most modern browsers (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge) allow you to manage cookie permissions, block third-party cookies, or clear existing cookies through their settings menu.
*   **Personalized Ads Opt-Out:** You can opt out of personalized advertising by Google at any time by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
*   **Industry Opt-Out Portals:** You can opt out of targeted advertising from participating ad networks via the [Digital Advertising Alliance](https://optout.aboutads.info/) or the [Network Advertising Initiative](https://optout.networkadvertising.org/).

*Note: Since CSRGO's developer utilities operate entirely client-side via JavaScript Web APIs, blocking or disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use the tools or read our educational guides.*
