## Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CSRGO ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit or interact with our website at [csrgo.com](https://csrgo.com).

### Information Collection

We prioritize data privacy and minimize data collection. We do not require account registration to view public information on this website. When you browse the website, standard web server logs may capture non-personally identifiable technical information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and timestamps.

### Cookies and Advertising

We may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to improve site performance and evaluate traffic patterns:

- **Analytics:** We may utilize privacy-friendly analytics tools to evaluate page visits and usage patterns.
- **Third-Party Vendors:** Third-party partners, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
- **Opt-Out:** Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or [AboutAds.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

### Information Security

We implement reasonable technical, operational, and organizational security measures to protect website operations from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
