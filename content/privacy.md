**Last Updated: November 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how CSRGO ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit or use our website at [https://csrgo.com](https://csrgo.com) and associated subdomains across the CSRGO digital ecosystem (including tools.csrgo.com, dsa.csrgo.com, and blog.csrgo.com) (collectively, the "Site").

We are dedicated to safeguarding your digital privacy and eliminating unwarranted data collection. Please read this policy to understand our data handling practices.

## 1. Zero Personal Information Collection

We want to be completely transparent: **CSRGO does not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII).**

*   **No Account Registration:** We do not provide or require user accounts, passwords, or authentication profiles. You can access all utilities, curricula, and publications immediately upon visiting.
*   **No Identity Harvesting:** We do not collect your name, physical address, phone number, payment details, or personal contact credentials to browse or use our services.
*   **No Comment Systems:** We do not operate public commenting systems that require you to submit personal identities or profile data.

## 2. Client-Side Architecture & Developer Tools Privacy

A fundamental pillar of the CSRGO platform is privacy by design:

*   **100% Client-Side Execution:** All browser-based utilities hosted across [tools.csrgo.com](https://tools.csrgo.com)—including JSON formatters, regular expression evaluators, cryptographic hash generators, token parsers, base64 encoders, and SVG tools—execute strictly inside your browser's local sandbox memory using native JavaScript Web APIs.
*   **Zero Server Transmission:** Your source code, input payloads, API keys, JSON objects, and sensitive data strings never touch a remote backend server, database, or cloud log. Execution occurs exclusively on your machine and ceases when you close the browser tab.

## 3. Hosting Infrastructure (GitHub Pages)

This Site is a static website hosted on **GitHub Pages** (operated by GitHub, Inc.).

*   **Technical Access Logs:** As our hosting infrastructure provider, GitHub may automatically collect standard web server access logs containing visitor IP addresses, browser user-agents, referring URLs, and request timestamps to maintain network integrity, troubleshoot system issues, and mitigate denial-of-service threats.
*   For detailed information on GitHub's data processing, please review the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## 4. Cookies and Web Storage

We do not use first-party tracking cookies to build personal behavioral profiles.

*   **Browser Local Storage:** We use client-side `localStorage` exclusively to persist non-identifiable user interface preferences—such as your preferred color theme (Dark Mode or Light Mode) and transient developer tool configurations. This data remains on your local device and is never synchronized to our servers.
*   **Managing Cookies:** Most web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through their settings preferences. You may disable cookies at any time through your browser controls.

## 5. Analytics and Third-Party Advertising

To sustain the ongoing development, hosting, and free availability of the CSRGO platform, we utilize trusted third-party services:

*   **Google Analytics:** We use Google Analytics to evaluate aggregated, non-personally identifiable traffic metrics (such as page visitation numbers, session durations, device categories, and broad geographical regions). Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this statistical data. It does not identify you personally to us.
*   **Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising:** We display advertisements served by Google AdSense to support the maintenance of this free ecosystem.
    *   Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the internet.
    *   Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
    *   Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or through the [Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out Tool](https://optout.networkadvertising.org/).

## 6. External Links & Services

Our Site contains links to external documentation, code repositories (such as GitHub), technical publications, and digital platforms. If you click on a third-party link, you will be redirected to that external provider's website. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites or services. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The CSRGO platform is designed for software developers, students, and general technology audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to our site, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational improvements, technological updates, or legal compliance requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of the policy. Continued use of the Site following any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated terms.
